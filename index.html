PRODUCT THINKING

How I think about AI products.

These are portfolio perspectives and frameworks — designed to show how I approach AI product decisions, not to imply that every concept below was implemented in a specific client project.

01

Agentic AI needs a product contract.

Define agent responsibilities, tools, state, escalation paths, success criteria and boundaries before optimizing prompts.

02

AI quality is a product metric.

Evaluate groundedness, retrieval quality, tool-call success, latency, cost and human acceptance — not just model output.

03

Human-in-the-loop is a design decision.

Risk, confidence and business criticality should determine when AI can act autonomously and when approval is required.

04

Architecture follows the workflow.

Start with users, decisions and operational constraints, then map data, services, agents and observability to the workflow.
